## 1. Purpose

M99 Knowledge Platform is the canonical product, supplier/manufacturer evidence, content, channel and governance platform for the M99 group. Dolibarr is an operational ERP/CRM/warehouse target, not the canonical master.

Canonical chain:

`ProductGroup -> ProductVariant -> SupplierOffer / SupplierMapping -> Evidence / Observation -> ChannelPresence -> ChannelContent -> ChannelPrice -> InventoryMapping`

External identifiers are mappings, not canonical identity. M99 identity follows `M99-` + digits. Identity states are `NEW / EXISTING / AMBIGUOUS / UNRESOLVED`; ambiguous identity requires a human decision.

## 2. Governance rules currently in force

- `ProductGroup` lifecycle: `draft -> active -> paused -> retired`; hard DELETE is exceptional and requires operator approval plus manual `DELETE` confirmation.
- Supplier and Manufacturer are separate governed roles. Operators may propose new sources, but proposals remain pending until Super Admin approval.
- Approved supplier/manufacturer sources may be used by operators; source identity/domain/deactivation remains Super Admin-controlled.
- Manufacturer evidence from a supplier page is evidence only. A supplier-provided brand string must not silently become an approved canonical Manufacturer.
- Supplier availability is external evidence and must never be interpreted as M99-owned physical stock.
- AI must not invent product facts. Official manufacturer evidence has higher priority than supplier evidence.
